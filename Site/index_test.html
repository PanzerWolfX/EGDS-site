Who We Are

Born from the ashes of a fragmented Europe, EuroGuardian Defense Solutions stands as the backbone of the European Federation’s security. Tasked with equipping the armed forces of Italy, the Benelux countries, Germany, Austria, and Switzerland, we develop every weapon, vehicle, and technology necessary to safeguard stability and advance operational dominance.

Our mission is to fuse cutting-edge engineering, artificial intelligence, and autonomous systems into a coherent, battlefield-ready suite of solutions that ensures the European Federation maintains strategic superiority across all domains of modern warfare.

Our Products

From the Praetor main battle tank and Streifer infantry vehicles, to Mirage bombers and Hekurani fighters, every EuroGuardian creation embodies precision, resilience, and tactical supremacy.

Our robotic troops, drones, and heavy artillery systems are integrated seamlessly into the Federation’s operational doctrine, providing commanders with unmatched flexibility, lethality, and real-time battlefield intelligence.
